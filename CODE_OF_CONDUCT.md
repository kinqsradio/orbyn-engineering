# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any
other project space, is expected to follow this code.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it the same way.
- Keep discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's personal data, credentials or private files.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

To report a problem to the maintainer, @kinqsradio, email hello@orbyn.dev.
For content that breaks GitHub's own rules, you can also use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
option. Reports are kept confidential as far as the project allows.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides on reports and may edit or remove content, warn someone, or
block them from the project, temporarily or permanently. The response depends on how
serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
